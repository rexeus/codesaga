Territories are now cut adaptively instead of at a uniform folder depth. The first cut is the packages (or the top-level folders), and a territory splits into its child folders when it is big (more than the smaller of 150 files and the larger of 30 files and a quarter of all files, or more than 40 % of all files) or when its folders have different main experts, and only when at least two folders hold 3 files or more; the leftovers become the `other` territory. `knowledge.territories` is now the tree: `territories` holds the first cut, and every territory carries its child `territories`, a `splitReason` such as "big: 52 files" or "src/api and src/ui have different experts", the `splitDetail` at which the split opens and `totalTerritories` (its children before `--limit`). A detail is how many splits are open, ordered by expertise gain first and then size; `maxDetail` is the finest one, replacing the per-detail lists. The recommended detail is the deepest one up to which expertise still separates folders, with no more territories than two per contributor active in 90 days (4 to 25). `--limit` cuts the first cut and the territories inside each one. `thresholds.territories` states the new constants (`bigShare`, `bigFilesShare`, `bigMinFiles`, `bigMaxFiles`, `mainExpertShare` replace `giantShare` and `giantSplitSteps`), and the `in-focus` badge now compares a territory with its siblings.
