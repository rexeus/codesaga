The dashboard gains a Stats section ("The code in numbers": histograms of file length, revisions and indentation complexity, languages, tests against code, the most changed files, style and habits) and an Achievements section (the repository's milestones as medallions, with the day reached and the progress toward the next). Territory cards open in place to their stats, who knows them, their badges with evidence and the territories inside them, which the Detail slider opens step by step; Expand all and Collapse all open or close every card. Badges wear the color of their category (Knowledge, Code, Activity) and only orphaned, knowledge island and knowledge fading keep a warning color. Every icon is now a Lucide icon, inlined into the page; the package ships its license in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`. The navigation scrolls sideways on a narrow screen.
