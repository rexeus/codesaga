The dashboard's charts can be read with the keyboard. Each chart is one tab stop: the arrow keys move between weeks, months or punch card cells, Home and End jump to the ends, and the tooltip a mouse would show appears and is read out by screen readers. Escape dismisses it. A key that cannot move the focus, such as an arrow at the edge of a chart, is left to the page, and resizing the window keeps the chosen point.
