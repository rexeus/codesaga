`deepDives.typescript` adds the functions of the code at HEAD. `functions` scores every function with cognitive complexity as the SonarSource whitepaper defines it and reports, for production code and tests apart, the functions per band (0–4, 5–9, 10–14, 15–24, 25 or more), the 50th and 90th percentile and the maximum, the share of functions and of code lines at 15 or more, the five hardest functions with their path and line, the functions by length (1–15, 16–30, 31–60, 61 or more non-blank lines), the long parameter lists and the deepest nesting. `complexityAndChange` shares the revisions that landed in files whose hardest function scores 15 or more and lists up to five files in the top decile of both complexity and revisions. `tests` counts test cases (`each` once), skipped, focused and `todo` tests with the files that hold a focused one, the cases by their direct assertions (0, 1, 2–3, 4 or more), snapshot assertions and type tests. `markers` counts `TODO`, `FIXME`, `HACK` and `XXX` comments, `@deprecated` tags and the share of exported declarations with a JSDoc block. Every territory's `typescript` gains `over15Share` and `maxComplexity`, and `thresholds.typescript` names the bands. `--limit` cuts the lists of hardest functions, hotspots and focused test files. The blocks are additive within `schemaVersion` 1.
