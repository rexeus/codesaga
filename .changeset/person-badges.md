Contributor badges gain a `category` and seven new kinds, all additive within `schemaVersion` 1. Every entry of `contributors[].badges` now carries a `category` of `focus` (all-rounder, specialist, keeper), `craft` (tidier, tester, documenter), `rhythm`, `collaboration` (reviewer) or `journey` (founder, steady, new-here, back-again), and a person's badges come in that category order. The new kinds come from the history alone: `night-owl` (22:00 to 05:00), `early-bird` (05:00 to 08:00) and `weekend-regular` (Saturday or Sunday) when a quarter of a person's human commits of the last year fall there on their own clock, with at least 40 such commits in 3 calendar months and withheld when the person's commits all say UTC while others' do not; `pair-partner` for 5 commits of the last year with a human `Co-authored-by` trailer; `long-hauler` for a first commit 3 years ago and a commit in each of the last 4 quarters; `explorer` for first commits in 3 territories within 90 days; and `toolsmith` when 30% of the human commits of the last year change only CI, container, manifest, lockfile or known tool configuration files (such as `vite.config.ts`, not `app.config.ts`). Badges describe and never rank or compare people. The thresholds are in `thresholds.badges`, and the dashboard shows each badge with an icon in the colour of its category.
