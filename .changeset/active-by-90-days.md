A contributor's `status` is `dormant` unless they committed in the 90 days before now, the rule behind `overview.contributors.active90`; `new` and `active` together are exactly those contributors. Before, `active` stretched over 183 days, so the dashboard called people active whose last commit was months old. Experts and the `active` flags stay at 183 days.
