`analyze` now fills `highlights` and the contributors' `badges`. Highlights are notable facts about the history and the team, ranked and capped at six: truck-factor alert, orphaned knowledge, anniversary, longest streak, busiest day, newcomers, biggest cleanup, quiet area, night owls, weekend share and rename record. Each appears only when it passes a threshold, and all thresholds are reported under `thresholds.highlights` and `thresholds.badges`. Contributor badges (all-rounder, specialist, cleaner, founder, keeper, tester, documenter, steady, welcome, returning) are positive or neutral and carry their rule as evidence; `reviewer` is not awarded yet. The terminal view shows the top three highlights and hides the Automation line when no bot or agent was detected.
